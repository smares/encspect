The fourth release. It is marked as a pre-release. 0.3.0 decoded the first lossy formats; 0.4.0 decodes every coding it reads except AAC and Windows Media Audio, which 0.5 adds, OptimFROG, which is read at header level, and the extensions of DTS (the full list is under "Known limits"). Musepack, DTS core, Speex and TAK are new, reads the codecs in the containers that known writers fill (MP4, Matroska, ASF and WAV), and adds coding graphs for every format whose data the parsers already read. Each stream now says whether its coding is lossless, lossy or hybrid, a unit whose checksum fails is left out of the measurements, and the correction files of WavPack and OptimFROG are read with their main files.

## What is new since 0.3.0

- **New decoders** (`--measure` and Decode Audio in the GUI; `--verify` decodes the codings that store a checksum over decoded audio), with the output compared with reference decoders (below):
  - **Musepack:** SV7 and SV8, and SV4 to SV6 as libmpcdec 1.2.6 decodes them, bit for bit that decoder's floating-point output. The parser reads every frame's resolutions, scalefactors and samples in every analysis ([musepack.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/musepack.md)).
  - **DTS core:** the subframes of every core frame are read in every analysis, and the decoder follows the specification, with the vector tables D.10.1 and D.10.2 (which the PDF does not hold) generated from FFmpeg 6.1.1 as format data. DTS extensions are not decoded ([dts.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/dts.md)).
  - **Speex:** narrowband, wideband and ultra-wideband with in-band stereo, bit for bit libspeex 1.2.1's fixed-point decoder with the perceptual enhancer on, as speexdec decodes; every frame's parameters are read in every analysis ([speex.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/speex.md)).
  - **WavPack 1 to 3:** the legacy `.wv` files are detected, read and decoded, with the audio CRC checked and the PCM held to the original decoders. The decoder follows libwavpack's BSD code ([wavpack.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/wavpack.md)).
  - **TAK:** codec 2 and 4, 8 to 24 bits, with the stream's MD5 compared where it has one (`--verify` decodes every TAK stream). The parser also walks every frame (frame count, header and data CRC-24s, bitrate per frame). The decoder was written from a description of the format by an implementer who read no TAK decoder ([tak.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/tak.md); [development.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/development.md#tak-decoding-clean-room)).
- **Correction files:** a WavPack `.wv` and an OptimFROG `.ofs` are looked for with their `.wvc` and `.ofc` beside the input, and the pair is reported together: consistent (lossless with its correction file), inconsistent, missing, none written or unchecked, in the same words for both formats. WavPack 4 and 5 pairs and WavPack 3's `.wvc` are decoded with their correction files and checked by the CRCs and the MD5; OptimFROG's pair is checked on headers and checksums, since it has no decoder. The GUI shows the pair in one detail window with a second tree ([correction-files.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design/correction-files.md); [cli.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/cli.md#the-correction-file-of-a-stream)).
- **The coding's class on the coding line:** every stream states whether its coding is lossless, lossy or hybrid, a property of the coding and never of the audio's history. The text report's `Coding:` line stands before `Verified:`, with the processor claim, the spectral cutoff and the low bits unused beside it; the GUI's streams line carries the class and an evidence line. `Verified: yes` now says what it rests on ("the decoded audio matches the stored checksums" or "the coded data matches the stored checksums"). The article "Coding Class" says what these figures can and cannot show.
- **Units whose checksum fails are left out of the measurements:** a unit whose checksum fails is still decoded and written, so the verification and the findings stay as they are, but its output, and the output that depends on its state, is withheld from the measurements and counted as "checksum failed" with a gap after it. Covered: FLAC and TTA, WavPack, Monkey's Audio and TrueHD units, MPEG audio, AC-3 and E-AC-3 frames, Ogg page and Matroska Cluster CRCs over the packets they cover, and container checksums over MPEG audio, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, TrueHD and MLP packets ([damaged-units.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design/damaged-units.md)).
- **Routes in containers:**
  - **MP4:** FLAC (`fLaC`), Dolby TrueHD (`mlpa`), PCM (`ipcm`, `fpcm` and QuickTime's PCM entries) and MPEG audio as packets, whatever the number of stretches the samples lie in; protected FLAC and TrueHD tracks are still named only.
  - **Matroska:** PCM (`A_PCM/*`); MPEG audio packets; header stripping for AAC, AC-3, DTS and MPEG audio (old mkvmerge releases write it by default); and `A_MS/ACM`, routed by the WAVEFORMATEX format tag (or the extensible sub-format GUID) to the codecs that WAV and ASF use, with the CodecPrivate shown as a WAVEFORMATEX node.
  - **ASF:** MP3 (format tag 0x0055) and AC-3 (0x2000), and fragmented media objects; the contents of script commands and markers are read.
  - **WAV:** the MPEG audio bursts of IEC 61937.
  - The table is in [formats/README.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/README.md).
- **Compression level of FLAC files:** the "Compression Level" node and statistic name the encoder and every level whose limits the frames fit ("libFLAC 7 or 8", "FFmpeg 5 to 12"), from the block size, the highest predictor and partition order and the channel decorrelation, with a confidence. libFLAC 1.1.0 to 1.5.0 are named from the vendor string (1.1.0 to 1.1.3 with a table of their own, blocks of 4,608 samples), and FFmpeg's native encoder from 16 releases (libavcodec 54.23 to 62.11). Levels that share their settings are named together; the stereo assignments of the adaptive levels 1 and 4 are a limit, not a sign. The tables are libFLAC's own `flac --help` output and FFmpeg run as a black box; 98 reference fixtures hold them, and the data file [flac-ffmpeg-levels.json](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design/flac-ffmpeg-levels.json) covers the other libavcodec versions by data, not run ([flac.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/flac.md#compression-level); article `flac-compression-levels`).
- **Nero's tags:** the MP4 parser reads the `tags` box that neroAacEnc, Nero Burning ROM and neroAacTag write, as the tag "Nero Tags": field lists, covers and per-chapter segments. A field, cover or chapter title that differs from the iTunes metadata or `chpl` beside it is a finding (`mp4.nero-field-differs`, `mp4.nero-cover-differs`, `mp4.nero-chapter-differs`) ([mp4.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/mp4.md#neros-tags-box)).
- **New coding graphs,** under the names, units and axes the other formats use:
  - **Prediction,** a new GUI heading after "Bit Distribution" (JSON concept `prediction`): FLAC's "Subframe Types", "Fixed Predictor Orders", "Highest Predictor Order" and "Coefficient Precision", with "Partition Orders", "Rice With 5-Bit Parameters", "Subframes With Wasted Bits" and "Block Size" under "Other"; "Highest Predictor Order" for ALAC and TrueHD and "Decorrelation Passes" for WavPack; TAK's "Average Predictor Order", "Highest Predictor Order", "Coefficient Precision" and "Channels With Wasted Bits".
  - **Compression Ratio** in every lossless format (FLAC, ALAC, Monkey's Audio, TTA, WavPack, OptimFROG, TAK, TrueHD, MLP and WMA Lossless): a statistics node and series per unit and per second, each unit's bits over the stream's PCM size. "Bitrate" and "Bitrate Over Time" are filed under "Bit Distribution" in DTS, FLAC, Monkey's Audio, Musepack, OptimFROG, Opus, Speex, TAK, TTA, TrueHD, MLP, Vorbis and WavPack.
  - **Stereo Coding** for TrueHD and MLP (from the matrices of the substream blocks, the two-channel presentation only), Monkey's Audio, TAK (none, add as left/side, subtract as side/right, mid/side, scaled and filter prediction) and the Musepack SV8 streams, and a flag series for the silent frames of mono Monkey's Audio streams.
  - **WMA:** "Block Sizes" and "Coded Bandwidth" per block, noise substitution per block, the subframe sizes of Professional and Lossless, and Voice's frame types.
  - **Opus:** "Modes", "Frame Sizes" and "Channel Coding" per stream packet. **Vorbis:** "Modes" per packet. **Speex:** "Narrowband Submodes", "Wideband Submodes" and "Ultra-Wideband Submodes" per frame.
  - **MPEG audio:** "Scalefactor Scaling" and "Global Gain". **AAC:** "Window Shapes", "TNS", "Pulse Data" and "Noise Substitution" per channel stream, "SBR" and "Parametric Stereo" per raw data block, and the numeric "Mid/Side Bands". **Musepack:** "Noise-Substituted Channels". **DTS core:** "Coded Bandwidth" and "Subframes With Block Switching".
  - **Intensity Stereo Bound per window:** in short and mixed blocks of Layer III the bound is read per window (see "Changed results").
  - The coding-graph survey of [coding-graphs.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design/coding-graphs.md#the-survey) lists what each format has and what is queued.
- **Encoder detection:** 24 new fixtures from old MP3 encoders under Wine (Plugger 0.4, 8hz-MP3 0.2b, M3E 0.99a and Fraunhofer's MP3 ACM codecs through acmenc), 60 files from the sample kit (Nero Burning ROM, neroAacEnc 1.0.0.2 to 1.5.4.0, qaac, iTunes, Winamp's Fraunhofer encoders and Windows 10's Media Foundation encoders). M3E's VBR modes name it from the text after the Xing header (medium confidence); the BladeEnc rule asks for a copyright or original bit in every frame, since Plugger and 8hz-MP3 code like BladeEnc. No rule exists for Plugger, 8hz-MP3 or the codecs' MPEG-2 files ([mpeg-audio.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/mpeg-audio.md#habits-of-encoders-without-tags)).
- **GUI:** "Show Only Audio" (a checkbox above the list and View, Show Only Audio) hides the files in which the analysis found no audio, by the detected format and never by file name; the status bar says how many files it hides. The streams line shows the coding class, the correction-file pairing and the evidence line ([gui.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/gui.md)).
- **JSON and CSV:** JSON (schema version 1, fields added only) gains `codingClass` after `codec`, `correction` in the stream, `companions` at the end of the report, `isCorrected` in the verification where true, `spectrum.coherence` in the measurements, and the withheld reason "checksum failed". The CSV gains `Coding Class` after `Codec`, `Correction File` after `Lossy Part Confidence` and `Inter-Channel Coherence From (Hz)` after `Side Cutoff (Hz)`, and its `Tags` column lists each tag with its version ([cli.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/cli.md)).
- **Inter-channel coherence** (`--measure`, the Audio panels, JSON and CSV): per frequency row, the magnitude-squared coherence of the pair of front left and right channels (or the two channels of a stream with no layout). The figure is the lowest frequency from which the coherence stays at 85 % or more to the end of the content, with the share of frames that show it in the band; where the parser states an "Intensity Stereo Bound" (MPEG audio, AAC, Opus), the median of the stated bound and the distance to the measured start stand beside it. It is a figure with a unit and a basis, not a verdict: near-mono content and a panned source read high too. Where the coherence rises slowly through 85 % the start is approximate. Text line `Inter-Channel Coherence`, a "Coherence" form of the GUI's Spectrum and a line in Figures; the article `inter-channel-coherence`; ten new `lossy-stereo-*` fixtures (TwoLAME 0.4.0, opusenc at 24 kbps and FFmpeg's `aac`) and two FAAC 1.40.0 streams ([spectral-analysis.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design/spectral-analysis.md#as-built-step-8)). AAC streams are measured through their FFmpeg decodes until the AAC decoder exists (0.5).

  - The decoder is also held to the TAK SDK's own read function over all 55 TAK fixtures, with its damage positions held to the frames the analyzer withholds.
  - New graphs: "Highest Predictor Order Over Time" and "Coefficient Precision Over Time" (the highest of each second) and "Highest Residual Mode" with its "Over Time" form. The channel table's paired types are not graphed yet.
- **Helix A1 fixtures:** three files from the re-coding fork of the Helix encoder (`external-helix-a1-vbr`, `-keep-gain-vbr` and `-no-tag-vbr`; the VBR files are 1.4 to 1.8 % smaller than the stock encode and decode to the same floats). Detection is unchanged: the tag names "Helix MP3 Encoder 5.24" and the tagless file is named at low confidence.
- **App icon:** the GUI executable has an icon (a Windows `.ico` with a small and a large form), and every window and the About window show it. It shows on Windows and X11 only: macOS has no app bundle and Wayland needs a `.desktop` file, which the release does not ship ([gui.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/gui.md)).
- **Empty media objects in WMA:** an ASF media object or a Matroska block of no bytes reaches the WMA parsers as a packet with no bytes: a superframe of no bytes in versions 1 and 2 (`wma.superframe-short`), a packet with `wma.packet-short` in Professional and Lossless, and dropped in Voice ([asf-wma.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/asf-wma.md)).
- **Cross-checks against reference decoders:** the PCM of each new decoder is compared with its reference: Musepack with libmpcdec, DTS core with FFmpeg and Speex with libspeex; the 33 encoder-written TAK fixtures decode to the signal Takc was given and to FFmpeg's samples bit for bit, and the decoder's output is compared with the TAK SDK's decoder by the records of Windows runs; WavPack 1 to 3 against the original decoders. The tolerances are in [development.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/development.md).
- **Background articles:** 245 articles in the GUI's "Learn More" and `--learn`, against 225 in 0.3.0.
- **Fixed since 0.3.0:**
  - A VBRI header frame in an MPEG-2 file (zero side information, 17 bytes) is no longer read as an audio frame at 144 kbit/s that shifts every later position; ffprobe skips it as the analyzer now does.
  - A finding of an MPEG audio, AC-3 or DTS stream mapped from a container points to a node of the tree, and an MPEG audio finding's frame node stops at its stretch.
  - The channel count in an MP4 AAC sample entry that disagrees with the stream is reported (`mp4.channels-differ`, info), for a plain mono stream whose entry states more and for an AudioSampleEntryV1; HE-AAC mono gets no finding, since decoders widen it to two channels.
  - A WavPack legacy or Monkey's Audio, TrueHD or other stream with a failing unit no longer feeds that unit's output to the measurements (above).
  - The Opus channel mapping text is built without the shared array pool.

## Changed results

Reports of existing files change in these ways. Goldens of the fixtures changed with them.

- **Verified wording:** `Verified: yes (...)` starts with what the checksums cover, as above; a script that matches the text needs the new form. `isLossy` stays.
- **Measurements of files with a failing unit:** the unit's samples and those that depend on it are withheld ("checksum failed") where the measurements used to count them (WavPack's muted blocks as digital silence, for example).
- **Layer III intensity stereo:** the decoder and the "Intensity Stereo Bound" series take the bound of each window of a short block, mixed or not, as ISO/IEC 13818-3 2.4.3.2 describes, where 0.3.0 took one bound for the three windows of a mixed block. No committed fixture changed: none has a mixed block with intensity stereo and a nonzero right channel. FFmpeg 6.1.1 and madplay 0.15.2b match the analyzer on the new hand-built files; minimp3 and mpg123 differ ([mpeg-audio.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/mpeg-audio.md#decoding)).
- **BladeEnc rule:** nine BladeEnc fixtures and one remux changed only their evidence text; files from Plugger and 8hz-MP3 are no longer named BladeEnc.
- **WavPack lossy and hybrid:** a hybrid or DualStream file without its correction file is lossy, with it lossless, and `StreamVerification.IsLossy` follows.
- **Coding graphs:** every golden with series changed, for the bitrate under "Bit Distribution" and the "Compression Ratio" series.
- **Measurement numerics:** K-weighting at sample rates other than 48 kHz keeps the 48 kHz filter's passband gain (libebur128 and FFmpeg read 0.2 LU above at 8 kHz and 0.03 below at 192 kHz), so loudness at 8 to 22 kHz changes by 0.1 to 0.3 LU; the cutoff, bandwidth and slope steps are rounded once and change by 0.1 dB in 5 to 10 % of the figures; the loudness range is rounded once; the float levels use Neumaier sums; the absolute gate is an exact literal ([spectral-analysis.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design/spectral-analysis.md), decision 13). 186 measurement goldens changed.
- **Layer III step size:** the decoder takes the gain and the scalefactor of a band as one combined power of two, where it rounded each separately as minimp3 does, so equal step sizes coded differently decode to the same floats. The decoded PCM of 166 of 476 MPEG audio fixture streams moved, by at most 9.7e-7 of full scale ([mpeg-audio.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/mpeg-audio.md#encoder-detection)).
- **WavPack 1 to 3, Compression Ratio:** the statistics node has its "Overall" figure only (the average bitrate over the PCM bitrate), with no "Lowest", "Highest" or series, since the decoder reads one bitstream sample by sample and adds no series ([wavpack.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/wavpack.md)).
- **PCM in Matroska and a failing Cluster:** only the sample frames of the packets of the Cluster whose CRC-32 fails are withheld from the measurements ([damaged-units.md](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design/damaged-units.md)).
- **A WavPack pair block decoded without its correction block:** a block whose correction block is missing, cut or fails its checksum is decoded as the lossy part (`wavpack.correction-block-missing`), counts as a failed block CRC and withholds its set from the measurements.
- **ASF, codings it does not route:** the message of `asf.codec-not-analyzed` says the coding "is not routed from ASF" to a codec parser (PCM, A-law, mu-law, DTS and MPEG Layer I and II among them); routing them is planned for 0.5.
- **JSON key renamed:** `isCorrected` in the verification (true where a correction file was applied) was `withCorrection` in builds of main before the 0.4 gate. 0.3.0 has neither, so only a reader written against such a build breaks; the key is `isCorrected` in 0.4.0, as in `isLossy`.

## What it reads

The formats and tags of 0.3.0, with these additions ([details](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/formats/README.md)):

- Musepack, DTS core, Speex and TAK: read, decoded and measured. WavPack 1 to 3: read and decoded. OptimFROG: with its correction file.
- FLAC, TrueHD, PCM and MPEG audio in MP4; PCM, A_MS/ACM and the stripped streams in Matroska; MP3 and AC-3 in ASF.
- Everything else as in 0.3.0: AAC, DTS-HD's lossless extension, the WMA codecs and OptimFROG are not decoded.

## Running it

Unpack the archive, then:

```sh
./encspect song.mp3 --measure    # text tree with the measurements; --json, --ndjson and --csv for the other outputs
./encspect --verify song.flac    # also compares the checksums over decoded audio
./encspect --version
./encspect-gui                   # the GUI; it also takes files and folders as arguments
```

On Windows these are `encspect.exe` and `encspect-gui.exe`. Options are in the [command line](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/cli.md) and [GUI](https://github.com/smares/encspect/blob/v0.4.0/docs/gui.md) documentation.

## The builds are not signed

The programs have no Developer ID or Authenticode signature and are not notarized (the .NET SDK ad-hoc signs the macOS executables, which Apple's notarization does not count), so each system asks before it runs them.

- **Windows:** SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose "More info", then "Run anyway". With Smart App Control on (Windows 11), Windows blocks programs it cannot judge, and unsigned ones among them, without a "Run anyway" option; Microsoft's "Smart App Control Frequently Asked Questions" says there is no way to bypass it for individual apps and that turning it off is the remedy.
- **macOS:** Gatekeeper refuses to start programs from a download that nobody it knows has signed. The GUI is a single executable, not an `.app` bundle. Follow Apple's route, described in the Mac User Guide page "Open a Mac app from an unknown developer" and the support article "Safely open apps on your Mac":
  1. Try to open the program once (double-click `encspect-gui`, or run `./encspect` in Terminal). macOS refuses.
  2. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click "Open Anyway". The button is there for about an hour after the attempt.
  3. Confirm with your login password, and open the program again.
  4. Repeat for the second program.

  The alternative in Terminal clears the quarantine attribute of the whole unpacked folder in one step. Use the folder's name for your download:

  ```sh
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine encspect-0.4.0-osx-arm64
  ```
- **Linux:** nothing is checked. The archive keeps the programs' executable bit; if a tool drops it, `chmod +x encspect encspect-gui` restores it.

## Linux libraries

As in 0.2.0. Both programs need glibc 2.27 or later; the builds are not for musl-based systems such as Alpine. The command line tool needs nothing more. The GUI also needs libstdc++ and libgcc_s; it draws with Skia and HarfBuzz, which are inside its file, but its Skia library links the system's libfontconfig, and its two windowing backends are managed code over the system's own libraries:

- **X11, the default** (also on a Wayland desktop, through XWayland): libfontconfig, libX11, libXrandr, libXi, libXext, libXfixes, libXcursor, libSM and libICE; libgtk-3 and libgdk-3 for the GTK file dialog when no desktop portal answers.
- **Wayland, experimental and opt-in** with `ENCSPECT_GUI_BACKEND=wayland`: libfontconfig, libwayland-client, libwayland-cursor, libwayland-egl, libxkbcommon, libgbm and libdrm, with EGL for drawing.
- **File dialogs:** on X11 the system dialog comes from the XDG desktop portal, then GTK, then Avalonia's own dialog; on Wayland it is the portal, then Avalonia's own dialog.

Without its display, a forced backend ends with one line naming `ENCSPECT_GUI_BACKEND`, `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` and `DISPLAY`, and exit code 1.

## Known limits

- **Not decoded:** AAC in all its object types, HE-AAC and xHE-AAC (0.5), the WMA codecs (0.5), OptimFROG (header level: its coding has no public description), the DTS extensions (XCh, X96, XXCH, XBR, XLL and DTS:X are read, not decoded) and the compressed codings of WAV and AIFF that are not PCM, A-law or mu-law (Microsoft and IMA ADPCM, GSM 6.10 and the rest are named by their format tag); the GUI's audio panels appear only for the decoded codings.
- **Partly decoded:**
  - MPEG audio: the MPEG-2 multichannel extension's separate bitstream is never read (such a stream decodes its two base channels), its multilingual channels are not decoded, and the data of mp3PRO and MP3 Surround is counted but not read, so a decoder of those reads the core or the downmix only.
  - DTS core: the channel arrangements 10 to 15 and the user defined ones are not decoded.
  - TAK: codecs 0, 1 and 3 (TAK 1.0) and bit depths other than 8, 16 and 24 are not decoded.
  - AC-3 and E-AC-3: frames at the reduced sample rates (`bsid` 9 and 10, `fscod2`) are read at header level only and not decoded, and no dynamic range control or downmix is applied.
  - Opus: mapping family 3 and unknown families are not decoded; lost frames are not concealed.
  - Vorbis: a setup header that states a very large codebook is not decoded.
  - Protected FLAC and TrueHD tracks in MP4 are named, not analyzed. Monkey's Audio files of versions 3.80 to 3.99 and 4.11 only; TTA audio format 1 only (format 2, a stream stored with a password, is refused); WavPack DSD is decoded to its 1-bit samples for the CRC and MD5 checks but not measured.
  - FLAC compression level: other FFmpeg versions than the 16 run (their tables are data, not run), libFLAC before 1.1.0 (1.0.3 writes no vendor string; 1.0.4 and 1.1.1 do not build) and after 1.5.0 are not named.
- **Not run in CI:** Dolby's decoder ran on one notebook, by hand, and its runs are records, not checks.
- **No playback and no tag editing:** by design.
- **Wayland:** native Wayland is experimental; the detail window's position is the compositor's choice.
- **Not run by a person:** the file dialogs, drag and drop and the clipboard on Wayland, and light and dark mode there.
- **Unsigned builds** (above).
